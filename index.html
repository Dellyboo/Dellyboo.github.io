Building the whole stack, not just the visible half.

I'm Mugisha Dear Duvet — most people know me as Dellyboo — a full-stack web developer and database specialist working with clients remotely from Japan. I like projects where the front end, the API and the schema underneath all have to work together cleanly — that's usually where the real engineering happens. Alongside web work, I build cross-platform mobile apps with Flutter and Firebase, and take on smaller jobs like web automation, debugging, logo design and AI-assisted content editing.

My approach is simple: understand the problem before writing code, keep the codebase easy to hand off, and communicate clearly at every step so there are no surprises at delivery.
